SEPARATE GODOT BROWSER PROTOTYPE

Adam’s Laboratory

A playable browser prototype of the Godot game: the laboratory, Adam’s Home, and the road between them. The surrounding world and lighting have been simplified for browser performance.

Play Adam’s Laboratory

WASD to walk · Mouse to look · Shift to sprint · E to interact · Esc for the menu.

A smaller world

Explore the lab and apartment, or use the pause menu to jump between them and open laboratory workstations. The desktop game and the existing web laboratory remain separate.

First load is approximately 150 MB and may take about 30 seconds. This prototype uses simpler lighting; performance depends on your computer.

Browser chemistry

The calculation engine runs locally in browser workers. Comparison checks agree with the desktop engine.
