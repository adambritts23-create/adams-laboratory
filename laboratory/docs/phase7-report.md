## 3. Derived quantities

Only branded prepared systems and branded, matching sweep results enter the transformation. Each plotted point additionally requires a branded successful point, passed scientific acceptance, matching system/input identity and matching scientific revision. Raw results are retained separately.

Let `m_j` be the reported aqueous molality or solid amount per kg water, `nu_ji` the retained signed source coefficient, and `T_i` the reported total of component `i`.

| Output | Definition | Units and restrictions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Log amount / concentration | `log10(m_j / (1 mol/kg-H2O))` | Aqueous molality and solid amount per kg water are explicitly distinguished by phase. Suppressed water/electron bookkeeping concentrations are unavailable. |
| Log activity | Stored `log10(a_j)` | Dimensionless. A present pure solid has log activity 0. An absent solid has no plotted actual activity; hypothetical saturation is not substituted. |
| Log dissolved component amount | `log10(dissolvedComponentAmounts[i] / (1 mol/kg-H2O))` | Requires a nonsuppressed component and nonnegative inventory coefficients. Excludes solid inventory. Does not claim intrinsic or necessarily saturated solubility. |
| Component distribution fraction | `nu_ji * m_j / T_i` | Dimensionless; free components use the identity coefficient. Includes solid inventory. Requires a nonsuppressed, nonnegative inventory basis and positive total. |
| Calculated pH | `-log10(a(H+))` | Dimensionless; requires the explicit proton component. |
| Calculated pe | `-log10(a(e-))` | Dimensionless; requires the explicit electron component. |
| Calculated Eh | `pe * R * ln(10) * T / F` | V relative to SHE, using the existing conversion constants and validated temperature. No new redox closure is inferred. |

Zero logarithms become `null` with `zero-log-undefined`; negative/unavailable amounts, nonfinite transformations and nonpositive fraction denominators become named gaps. No epsilon substitution, clamping, invented floor or fabricated finite equilibrium value is used. Signed proton/electron balance semantics are not silently reinterpreted as nonnegative distribution inventories.

## 4. Transformation verification

Tests compare each log series directly with accepted raw values; independently check pH and pe/Eh signs and units; compare dissolved inventory with raw dissolved amounts; and check fractions sum to one including the solid. Present/absent solid activity semantics and zero solid amount are checked. Unsupported targets/types, cloned untrusted results and invalidated sweeps are rejected. These are mathematical and boundary tests, not replacement oracle data.

The Phase 6 user-defined record test now verifies identical derived values through the normal repository/preparation/sweep path, the `user-defined` provenance flag, and invalidation when that species is deliberately removed. Its additional removal setup explicitly selects its Ag/Cl discovery elements; no scientific fixture or tolerance changed.

## 5. Unsupported outputs

Predominance, relative activity and hydrogen affinity are visibly disabled. General solubility semantics, signed-balance fractions, arbitrary reaction-affinity outputs, phase-boundary refinement and 2D/3D maps are not claimed. Gas identities can still be browsed, but gas equilibrium calculations remain unsupported by preparation.

## 6. Continuous workspace

System and Calculation are views of the same `LaboratorySession`. The System view keeps element discovery, source component/redox forms and chemical species as separate concepts. Source descriptions are reused; no new component forms are inferred from periodic-table selection. H+, e- and solvent water retain their special roles.

The Calculation view has supported conditions in a sidebar and output selection, series controls, graph and inspection alongside it. Narrow layouts stack these sections. There is no save/reopen handoff. Imported database loading remains local source selection, not an intermediate plot-file workflow.

The supplied Spana/Hydra images were treated as scientific/workflow references only: component forms, numeric axes, fixed conditions and labeled chemical curves. No yellow panels, Windows modal style or old file-dialog workflow were copied.

## 7. Visibility versus chemical membership

`visualizationState.plot` stores output, visible IDs, theme and live-recalculation preference. Visibility does not alter the chemical system, scientific revision or canonical sweep. Search, phase filtering, show-all and hide-all work in the calculation workspace. Candidate series may be selected before the first run; this does not claim those candidates have accepted calculated values.

Actual membership is changed deliberately in System, increments the revision and requires recalculation. A previously accepted graph can remain visible only with its old identity and stale label. Newly added series can be enabled explicitly or with Show all; they do not silently inherit another curve's visibility identity.

## 8. Renderer architecture

`PlotWorkspace` connects the validated transformation to `ScientificPlot`. The renderer, geometry and export modules do not import solver/preparation modules; a permanent test checks that boundary. SVG uses actual numeric coordinates, sampled straight segments, axes and direct labels for up to eight visible curves. Distinct catalog-index colors are used for the first six series, with a repeating palette thereafter. Colors remain stable during filtering/hiding within a result. Many-curve label collision avoidance remains a presentation limitation.

Navigation includes zoom, horizontal drag, X/Y pan buttons and reset. It never changes sampling or solves new chemistry. The full canonical sweep is retained; there is no silent decimation.

## 9. Missing-point behavior

Every requested sample remains in the derived sequence, including failed and not-run samples. Each missing quantity has `value: null` and a reason. SVG splits runs at gaps and draws isolated accepted samples as circles; it cannot join two accepted points across a missing point. Tests include an explicit renderer-only two-singleton gap layout, not a fabricated equilibrium fixture.

An entirely failed new run preserves the old accepted plot, with current-run failures accessible separately. Partial accepted sweeps show their own failure gaps and `completed-with-failed-points` status. Invalidated stale sweeps cannot become new scientific plots.

## 10. Live controls

Precise numeric fields retain supplied numbers. Optional positive-total sliders operate in log10 numerical total, with the displayed slider range documented; numeric input supports other ranges. Activity sliders expose supported pH/log-activity/Eh values. Axis min/max/count remain axis controls rather than fixed pH controls.

`liveRun.js` delays edits 350 ms, aborts superseded work and supplies an `isCurrent` generation predicate. App orchestration uses the existing `prepareSessionPoint`, `createSweepDefinition` and `runSweep` functions. There is no second equilibrium/sweep implementation. Turning live mode off cancels pending work.

## 11. Recalculation and identity

Scientific edits immediately increment revision and invalidate current results. `lastPlot` retains both the old prepared system and its immutable sweep. The visible banner says conditions changed/updating or result stale. Figure metadata and printed conditions continue to describe that old snapshot.

The controller checks generation across async preparation and execution; the session reducer checks branded result, revision, system and sweep identities before committing. A new accepted result replaces the snapshot atomically. New preparation failures and failed current-run point diagnostics are shown separately. Cancelling through the UI prevents superseded results from committing; the underlying engine still represents the full cancelled/not-run sequence. Deserialization discards persisted calculated results and retained plots until recalculated.

## 12. Conditions and provenance

The figure itself prints validated temperature, declared pressure, activity model, ionic-strength assumption and every fixed component condition with mode/quantity/units. Metadata includes axis definition, output formula/units, system/sweep identity, revision, solver method, source identity and warnings.

Automatic ionic strength is labeled **not evaluated**, not falsely reported as a computed ionic strength. Fixed zero is declared explicitly. Missing source reference pressure remains unknown even though calculation pressure is declared 1 bar. Existing engine-scope warnings are retained; output transformation is supplied by the separate Phase 7 layer.

## 13. Exact inspection and crosshair

Pointer position maps to the nearest actual sample, not an interpolated equilibrium. Click pins that sample; hover temporarily selects the nearest sample. A numeric index provides keyboard inspection. Values and coordinates use 17 significant digits, with explicit unavailable reasons. Crosshair bounds follow the actual SVG plot region independently of the number of condition lines.

Browser click verification pinned sample 27 at pH `8.1600000000000001`; the source carbonate log amount was `-5.1699465255787347` for the original 0.001 mol/kg total. Unit tests verify exact lookup and numeric pH/Eh axis labeling. Standalone hover movement was not independently verified through automation: the available wrapper exposed click/drag but not the separate move API. No claim of an independently observed hover-only state is made.

## 14. Export

Export SVG produces a standalone figure with printed conditions and machine-readable metadata, including current versus result revision and a stale flag. All source-derived text is XML-escaped. Export numerical JSON includes the full prepared system, complete canonical sweep, derived series, raw quantities, all gap positions, visibility/view state, method and provenance.

The SVG explicitly does not claim to be a complete reproducibility package. JSON should accompany it. No PNG/CSV implementation was necessary because SVG and JSON satisfy the prioritized figure/data boundary. Browser export controls were exercised without console errors; exact package fields and gaps are unit-tested. Importing exported calculated results as trusted application state is unsupported.

## 22. Scientific and practical limitations

Ideal activities only; 25 C with current retained thermodynamic data; declared 1 bar; mol/kg water; supported direct basis; at most one candidate pure solid. No general independence/completeness certification, arbitrary redox closure, nonideal activity models, gas fugacity systems, density conversions, temperature/pressure extrapolation, multiple simultaneous solids, grids, Pourbaix maps or 3D surfaces.

Plots reflect the selected restricted reaction set, not every chemically possible species. Straight lines connect accepted samples only and do not establish exact phase boundaries. Source record provenance, unknown fields and user-defined unverified status remain explicit. Reloading/changing the database starts a new session. Plot image export alone is not sufficient for full numerical reproduction. Six colors repeat for larger series sets, and direct label placement is intentionally simple.
