## Interaction

Calculate switches the same surface to Results when a new calculation returns. Setup is hidden, the work surface is brought below the sticky navigation, and the graph occupies the primary area. Edit setup preserves inputs and the mounted plot (including existing focus/view state). Background updates while editing do not close the editor unless an explicit calculation is pending.

Results show conditions from their calculation snapshot. Edited inputs cannot relabel an older graph: retained results explicitly say OLD conditions. Preparation errors remain visible near setup, with a Review setup action when viewing retained results. All-failed runs show an explicit no-accepted-values alert. Partial runs retain gaps and point diagnostics. Existing export implementations and exact numerical precision remain unchanged.

On desktop, species buttons sit to the right of the 1D graph. Clicking a species emphasizes its existing curve and de-emphasizes others; its direct label remains identifiable. Clear focus restores the other curves. On narrow screens the legend wraps above the full-width graph. Inspection, direct labels, zoom/pan/reset, expansion and exports remain in the existing plot architecture.
