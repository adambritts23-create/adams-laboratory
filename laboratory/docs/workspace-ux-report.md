9. **Partition formula.** For ordinary analytical component i with supplied total Tᵢ, the solid component amount is Sᵢ = Σₛ νᵢₛ nₛ over accepted positive solid amounts only. Displayed solid percentage is 100 Sᵢ/Tᵢ; dissolved percentage is 100 Dᵢ/Tᵢ, where Dᵢ is the accepted result’s dissolved component amount. Each solid allocation is also shown. These are component partitions, not solubilities.

10. **Stoichiometry and authority.** νᵢₛ is read from the prepared source product’s component coefficients, using the same branded system/input/accepted result as the selected beaker. Fe₂O₃ uses coefficient 2; Ni(OH)₂ uses 1. No second equilibrium calculation, inferred precipitation, candidate counting or neutral-aqueous counting. Water/proton are omitted; activity-controlled components, unresolved tiny totals, signed inventories or invalid balances are unavailable. Fractions are not clamped or renormalized. Small positive shares display <0.1% rather than a misleading zero; detailed amounts and residuals remain available.
