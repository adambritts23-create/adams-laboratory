The Geiger-style tick is a short excerpt of the circuit-breaker recording: atmospheric Foley, not a recorded/calibrated radiation instrument. Pump/compressor and pressure-cooker recordings are repurposed as fictional facility machinery and pneumatic ambience.

Processing: mono conversion, frequency filtering, gain normalization, 24 kHz PCM conversion, loop crossfades or short event excerpts. The derivative JSON records source windows, filters, durations and levels. Original MP3s and license-page evidence remain in the ignored source directory.

Existing footsteps/jump/landing and narrative alarm remain locally synthesized. The old synthesized room clip is retained for API compatibility but is not played as the ambience bed. The older Phase 5 recordings remain covered by their existing manifest; this pass uses the new recorded ambience assets.

Character assets remain the existing Quaternius CC0 humanoid meshes, outfits, hair and skeletons, edited offline in Blender. No additional external character assets were imported. Adam photographic references were supplied by the user; Axel uses his existing supplied laboratory-reference texture. These photographs are not represented as CC0 assets.
